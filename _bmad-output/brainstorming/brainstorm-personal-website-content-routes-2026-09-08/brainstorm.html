Ritual innovation

Arrival is a gentle threshold.

A visitor chooses where they feel drawn, not which category they must consume. Motion, sound, hover surprises, and an imaginary ticket remain optional; every ritual has a direct text equivalent.

Look at the board

Scan a destination name, its plain-language meaning, a current notice, and one invitation to step closer.

Suggested gesture

Sit on the bench

Read only the welcome and a few notices. The station clock marks pace softly—never a countdown.

Suggested gesture

Step into the house

Meet the pet, enter a safe personal room, and follow a bedside object only when curiosity naturally occurs.

Project depth model

At the field gate, story comes first.

Visitors choose how deeply to explore. No completion meter suggests that every layer must be taken.

Fast path · required entrance

01 · Story

Every project begins with What is this? and Why did it need to exist? Context arrives in the order a human would understand it.

Optional path

02 · Atmosphere

Stand inside the project's weather: visual language, feeling, references, choices, and lived experience.

Optional path

03 · Craft

Enter the work shed: constraints, architecture, decisions, implementation, engineering details, and lessons in that order.

Reusable project pattern: /projects/[slug] → Field Gate → story → atmosphere and/or craft → field note → fields, related project, station board. If the deeper shed is quiet, say so honestly; never present an empty shell.

Atmospheric treatment
Navigation safeguards

Comfort is a system requirement.

Warmth never substitutes for practical trust. The engineering is intentional even when the surface feels playful.

Readable wandering

Small You are here markers, literal labels, stable URLs, accessible breadcrumbs, search as “Find a place or note,” and a complete all-routes page.

No hidden gates

Curiosity rewards are nonessential; visible alternatives reach the same information. Pet, ticket, surprise, and quiet pace are optional.

Every screen belongs

Small screens prioritize board, current location, route title, and return links. Keyboard and assistive technology receive the same tree through headings, landmarks, links, and text.

Honest quiet

Sparse sections explain what may arrive without invented filler. Unfinished trails name their state. Reduced motion removes movement, sound, and decorative timing while preserving all routes.

Three stable exits

From any route: board, home/platform, and immediate parent place. Leaving feels like stepping back onto the platform.

Trust at the letterbox

Contact begins with intent and ends with what happens next and when. A no-pressure exit remains available before any message is sent.
