# Website Intent

## Core intent
Create a personal world that reveals who LinCie is and what they are capable of, with the atmosphere—not a conventional portfolio—as its heart. The station is the doorway; the work is encountered as part of a living journey.

## Emotional promise
Offer visitors a warm, friendly, safe place to slow down, feel recognized, and leave happier. Communicate: “Welcome. You made it here. Take your time.” Hold hope without denial: visitors may continue at their own pace, slow progress counts, what they tend may become beautiful, and their journey is not over.

## Atmosphere
Cute, soft, warm, beautiful, calming, and subtly alive. The feeling is a small bright pause: comfort, breathing room, and gentle optimism rather than noise, pressure, or sentimentality. Warm morning light, soft wind, grass, fresh rural air, and an expansive landscape establish renewal and openness.

## Metaphor/world direction
A Japanese rural train station and station house at the edge of an expansive landscape: a welcoming place to set out, return, rest, and recognize how far one has traveled.

- The train is the path; the tracks are the continuity and direction of the journey.
- Rice fields are seeds planted and projects built over time.
- The mountain is the future LinCie is growing toward: meaningful, patient aspiration rather than an obstacle.
- The forest is the unexplored journey: curiosity, learning, inner depth, and paths not yet taken.
- Cicadas are small, persistent voices of joy continuing through changing seasons.

## Visitor experience
The arrival view should immediately establish relaxation, identity, and vocation through the station board, station, and surrounding landscape. Visitors should feel welcomed rather than evaluated, recognized rather than watched, and safe to wander without a prescribed route, pressure, testing, or fear of getting lost. The station’s recognition should feel like a gentle gaze that says they belong and may take their time.

## Identity/capability direction
LinCie is a software engineering trainee whose personal identity and capability should be felt together from arrival. The station’s visual grammar carries the identity; connected routes, meaningful signals, purposeful interactions, coherent navigation, and precise responses quietly demonstrate engineering ability. The engineered layer stays subtle beneath the cozy surface and is discovered rather than announced.

## Design principles
- Personal world first; portfolio second.
- Warmth is the center; subtlety is the method.
- Make care tangible through precise, calculated, understated interactions.
- Let joy live in carefully crafted details such as swaying grass and intentional light.
- Keep animation and decoration restrained, purposeful, and non-overwhelming.
- Use genuine, direct, friendly, reassuring messaging—the voice of a real host, not polished marketing language.
- Preserve free self-expression and a world that could not belong to just anyone.

## Boundaries for future content work
Do not define detailed portfolio content in this direction. Future work should serve the personal world and its atmosphere rather than turn it into a generic showcase. Treat projects and capabilities as part of LinCie’s cultivated, unfolding journey; keep the work secondary to the welcoming experience and do not let content introduce performance pressure, noisy excess, or polished emptiness.
