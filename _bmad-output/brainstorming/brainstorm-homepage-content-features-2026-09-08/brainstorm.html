The departure board, resolved

The final synthesis

The session’s strongest ideas gathered into one calm, legible arrival composition.

“The homepage is not a sales funnel or a résumé cover; its primary product is a calm, legible feeling of arrival.”

Chosen homepage blueprint

Arrival

Greeting and identity: Welcome. You made it here. Take your time. Identify LinCie and software engineering plainly.

Board

Visible station board with five equal routes: About Me, Projects, Things LinCie offers, Personal thoughts, and Ways to connect.

Signal

One compact current signal: what LinCie is making, learning or wondering about, and what brings comfort.

Gestures

Three optional invitations: look at the board, sit for a moment, or step into the station house.

Return

Stable map, quiet, and return controls; every destination keeps the route back to the station obvious.

Priorities

01Semantic navigation and direct text equivalents
02Reduced-motion / quiet behavior
03Current content signals and clear route exits
04Elaborate ambient interactions, later

Non-negotiables

  • warmth, friendliness, safety, freedom to wander
  • low cognitive pressure and quiet space
  • no autoplay sound, forced tour, countdown, or hidden essential routes
  • keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, and low-bandwidth visitors receive the same information
  • playfulness rewards attention; it never requires puzzle-solving

A visitor can describe LinCie, name one thing they make, choose a destination, and leave feeling lighter.

Minimum viable homepage: greeting · identity · station board · one current signal · a clear invitation to wander or rest.
